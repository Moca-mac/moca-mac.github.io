Moca

A beautiful calendar app centered on the monthly view. Infinite scrolling through time, seamless integration with your system calendars, and effortless event creation.

Infinite Monthly Scrolling

Navigate endlessly through months in a single, fluid vertical scroll. No rigid month boundaries—view recent and upcoming schedules at a glance, perfect for lighter schedules.

System Calendar Integration

Loads events directly from your built-in calendars. Supports Google Calendar and iCloud with zero setup. Just works with your existing System Preferences.

Natural Language Events

Type a sentence like "Shopping on Saturday from 11am-1pm" and watch it become a fully formed event. Dates, times, and details parsed automatically.

Full-Featured Essentials

Full-screen mode, reliable notifications, drag-and-drop to move events (option-drag to copy). Designed for clarity and efficiency on macOS.

How It Works

Step 1

Install Moca. It instantly syncs with your System calendars—no accounts or configurations needed.

Step 2

FAQ

Is setup required?

No. Moca pulls from your existing System calendars (Google, iCloud) automatically.

Best for whom?

Ideal for monthly overviews and lighter schedules. Less suited for dense weekly views.

Does it support notifications?

Yes, full notifications plus drag-and-drop event management.
